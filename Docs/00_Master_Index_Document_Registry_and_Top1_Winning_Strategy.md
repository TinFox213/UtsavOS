# **3\. Core Innovation Pillars Built into AuraPlan AI (Practical & High-Impact Ideas)**

1. **Multi-Archetype & Multi-Currency Intelligence (INR ₹ / USD \$ / EUR € / GBP £):**  
   * **Weddings:** Supports multi-event ceremonies (Mehendi, Sangeet, Muhurtham/Ceremony, Reception, Cocktail) with per-function guest splits and ritual-specific vendor checklists.  
   * **Birthdays:** Milestone birthdays (1st, 18th, 21st, 50th), kids' theme parties, and surprise parties with entertainment, cake sizing math (grams per guest), and return-gift calculators.  
   * **Community Events:** College cultural/tech fests, hackathons, charity fundraisers, neighborhood cultural melas, and townhalls with sponsor tier tracking, stage/AV run-of-show, volunteer shifts, and crowd safety permits.  
2. **Parametric Budget & Per-Capita Feasibility Engine:**  
   * Instantaneously detects impossible budgets (e.g., 400 guests for a Luxury Wedding on ₹50,000 or \$500) and computes the exact **Minimum Viable Per-Capita Threshold**, offering 1-click **AI Value-Engineering Swaps** (e.g., switching peak Saturday evening hotel ballroom to morning boutique mandapam/garden \+ seasonal menu to save 38%).  
   * Includes **Hidden Cost & Tax Shield**: Automatically reserves configurable contingency (8%) and GST/Service Taxes so users never face last-minute financial shocks.  
3. **Critical Path Method (CPM) Timeline & Live Crisis Re-Scheduler:**  
   * Generates both a **Macro Countdown Roadmap** (`T-180 days` to `Event Day`) and a **Minute-by-Minute Event Day Run-of-Show**.  
   * **Live Delay & Weather Ripple Simulator:** If the user inputs *"Caterer delayed by 45 mins"* or *"Outdoor rain forecast at 6:00 PM"*, the engine shifts dependent timeline blocks and uses Gemini to generate an instant guest-engagement and vendor-rerouting protocol.  
4. **Vendor Checklist, Quote Comparator & Smart WhatsApp/Email RFP Generator:**  
   * Tracks 12 vendor categories with 120+ actionable checklist items, payment tranches (`Booking Advance`, `Mid-Milestone`, `Event-Day`, `Post-Event Holdback`), and AI-generated negotiation scripts.  
5. **Visible GenAI Transparency ("Prompt X-Ray Drawer" \+ Dynamic Diff Engine):**  
   * Specifically designed for Section 3 of the Video Submission Guide: clicking **"Inspect AI Prompt & Dynamic Proof"** reveals the live prompt payload sent to Gemini, the structured JSON response, and a **Compare Runs** view that highlights every single field that dynamically changed when the user modified event parameters.
